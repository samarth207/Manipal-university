Advantages Of Our Online BCom

Learn on Your Schedule

Having to log in and study from anywhere, at any time, is one of the most significant advantages of our online BCom programs. This provides learners with the pace to balance their education, work, family, and other commitments.

Career Advancement Without a Break

An online BCom is a valuable career enhancement plan, as it enables individuals to continue their employment without putting it on hold while still allowing them to gain a postgraduate qualification easily.

Cost-Effective and Time-Saving

In comparison with traditional BCom programs, online BCom programs are often more cost-effective. Additionally, students can save a significant amount by eliminating commuting costs and focusing entirely on their coursework.

Access to Global Learning and Networking

With an online BCom, learners are acquainted with fellow students and faculty from around the world. This builds learning about new cultures, fosters strong global networking, and also involves working on cross-cultural projects.

Same Degree, Same Value

Reputed universities offer online BCom programs with the same curriculum and faculty as their on-campus programs. You earn the same accredited degree, which holds equal value in the job market.

Learn More About Our BCom Programs (FAQs)

What are the best career options after completing BCom course? +

After completing a BCom degree, you can pursue various career paths including Accountant, Financial Analyst, Tax Consultant, Auditor, Investment Banker, Business Consultant, Cost Manager, Wealth Manager, and many more. You can also opt for higher studies like MBA, M.Com, CA, CS, or CMA.

Does an online BCom have value? +

Yes, our online BCom degrees are UGC-entitled and from NAAC A+ accredited universities, making them equivalent to traditional degrees. They are recognized by employers and suitable for both job opportunities and higher education. The flexible learning format allows you to balance studies with work or other commitments.

What is the average salary after completing the BCom course? +

The average salary for BCom graduates in India ranges from ₹3-6 lakhs per annum for entry-level positions. With experience and additional certifications, this can increase to ₹8-15 lakhs or more. Salaries vary based on job role, industry, location, and your skill set.

Will I earn a degree from an accredited university? +

Yes, you will earn a UGC-entitled degree from NAAC A+ accredited Manipal Universities. The degree is at par with on-campus degrees and is recognized by employers, universities, and government organizations across India and internationally.

Who should pursue an Online BCom Course? +

An online BCom is ideal for recent 12th pass students, working professionals seeking career advancement, individuals preparing for professional courses like CA/CS/CMA, aspiring entrepreneurs, and anyone interested in building a career in commerce, finance, accounting, or business management.
