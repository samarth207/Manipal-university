Learn More About Our BCom Program (FAQs)

Are flexible payment options available for these courses? +

Yes, Online Manipal offers flexible payment options including no-cost EMI starting at INR 2,750 per month, semester-wise payment of INR 16,500, and full program fee payment of INR 99,000 with applicable concessions.

Is a scholarship available for these courses? What is the eligibility criteria? +

Yes, Manipal University Jaipur offers exclusive scholarships of up to 20% for various categories including Divyaang (20%), Defence Personnel (20%), Manipal Alumni (20%), Government Employees (10%), and Merit-Based (10%).

What is the admission process for the online BCom degree? +

Step 1: Fill in your basic, education & work experience-related details and pay the application fee. Step 2: Pay the admission fee for the first semester/year or full program. Step 3: Upload supporting documents and submit your application. Step 4: The university will evaluate your documents to confirm your admission.

What are the best career options after completing BCom? +

After completing a BCom degree, you can pursue careers in Cost Management, Taxation & Compliance, Auditing & Assurance, Wealth Management, Corporate Finance, Financial Analytics, Investment Advisory, Business Strategy, and more. You can also pursue higher studies like MBA, CA, CS, or CMA.

How are the classes conducted? +

Classes are delivered through live sessions and recorded video lectures via the LMS. Students can also engage in conversation in the discussion forum. The advanced learning system includes Flash Cards, Mind Maps, Scenario-Based Learning, and 10-Min Summaries.

Are online degrees by Online Manipal credible and valid? +

Yes, all online degree programs offered through Online Manipal are UGC-entitled, making them equivalent to on-campus degrees. MUJ is NAAC A+ accredited and ranked among India's top 60 universities by NIRF.

What are the BCom specializations/electives available? +

The online BCom from MUJ offers 4 electives: Banking & FinTech, Business Accounting & Taxation, Accounting with AI, and Economics. Students choose their specialization during their studies.
